# Gateways: review notes, October 2026

Where each entry in `src/data/gateways.ts` came from, how sure the checker was, and what is still doubtful.
Written from the first batch of 20 places. Distances are road distances from open routing data; drive times are the
routing service's time multiplied by about 1.5 (about 1.8 in the hills) or a source's typical time. Every entry should be
re-checked about once a year. Corrections made by hand after the research are listed under "Changes made at review".

## Changes made at review

- **darjeeling**: the bus station found was in Siliguri (64 km), not Darjeeling's own, so it is shown as the nearest bigger town instead.
- **manali**: Chandigarh Junction is the practical railhead but not the nearest; Joginder Nagar (narrow gauge, few trains) was added as the nearest, and the Kullu-Manali airport note was shortened so it won't go stale.
- **ooty**: Mettupalayam set to 51 km / about 2 h (sources give 46 to 52 km); the routing service said 59 km.
- **mysuru**: Kannur airport removed (a long ghat drive); Mysuru and Bengaluru remain.
- Every list is sorted nearest first, and any in-town hub (0 km) is shown as 1 km / 5 min.

Open doubts and decisions:

I listed only Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru (also supported by RedBus/Rome2Rio search snippets). The two OSM stands are 0.4 km (KSRTC) and 0.8 km (private) from the place point; km shown as 1 and minutes as 5 because OSRM rounds to 0. ### goa (confidence: medium)

Open doubts and decisions:
- Goa is a whole state; the place point (15.2993, 74.124) is inland in central Goa, so every km/minutes figure is to that point. Beach areas in the far north are closer to Mopa/Thivim, and in the south to Dabolim/Madgaon. The UI should not call any single Goa hub 'the' nearest without this context.
- Airport service levels: both are tagged 'most' as a judgement (Wikipedia tables: Dabolim 9 airlines / 25 destinations incl. international, 6.13M passengers Apr 2025-Mar 2026 per AAI figures cited there; Mopa 13 airlines / 35 destinations, but that table is marked 'needs update, July 2025' and includes seasonal charters). If 'most' is reserved for Delhi/Mumbai/Bengaluru-scale hubs, change both to 'more'. I did not open a live airline schedule; AAI's own flight-schedule page was not readable.
- Dabolim civil-flight restriction (no civil flights during Navy training hours, roughly 08:30-13:00 on five days a week) comes from the Wikipedia article only; I kept the note vague on purpose.
- Rail: Curchorem/Sanvordem (SVM, 11 km, nearer to the central point) deliberately left out. Only a handful of trains seem to stop there (Goa Express 12779 daily, Poorna Express weekly, Kulem passenger trains) and I could only confirm that from aggregators, not NTES/Indian Railways. Karmali (KRMI, 47 km, closest to Panaji) also left out; Thivim is the more clearly main North Goa station, but Wikipedia's 'about 38 halting trains' for Thivim is a single-source claim.
- Rail: no road numbers in candidates.json for Thivim, so km/min are my own OSRM run (60.1 km, 67 min x1.5 = 100). Panaji bus stand is also my own OSRM run (50.2 km, 56 min x1.5 = 85). Others are from candidates.json (MAO 29.1/34, GOI 52.3/69, GOX 75.1/92, Margao KTC 30.7/38).
- Bus: only Margao and Panaji Kadamba stands listed (KTCL's own stands page names 16 stands; Mapusa and Vasco also run interstate routes). Operator set to 'government' because only KTCL is confirmed; private overnight buses also serve Panaji/Margao but I did not source that. goesTo comes from KTCL's 'Routes operated by KTCL' page, which gives route names and via-points only (no timings). Panaji is listed as a start only for the routes shown; Mumbai/Pune start at Margao (Mapusa has the Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru routes).
- bookingUrl https://www.goakadamba.com opened (HTTP 200) and is named as the online booking site on KTCL's own site and in the KTCL citizen charter (goa.gov.in, Jan 2026). ktclgoa.com also loads.
- Belagavi (IXG, 139 km) not listed as an airport: it has few flights and is farther than both Goa airports.
